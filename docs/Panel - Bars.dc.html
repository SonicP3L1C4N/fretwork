Bars strip (ruler)

The 8b ruler: ticks and whitespace, section names flush-left in the serif italic at each section start (tall tick), minor bars as short ticks with sparse numerals below. The playing bar is the one printed element — a magenta lozenge riding the ruler line. The caret (selected bar) is an outlined magenta box around its numeral. Sits under the score, full window width; sections come from the score, playhead from the transport.

Build notes: 46px per bar at this density; tall ticks (12px) at section starts, short ticks (6px) elsewhere; numerals every 4th bar. Lozenge is 28×20 rx 10, slides continuously with the playhead. If the score has no sections (hasSections false), drop the name row and keep uniform short ticks. Ground is --color-neutral-200 against the paper score above.
